## 🚀 About Nzagha

**Nzagha is Long Africa, Permanent Capital Based in Biafra.**

We build, buy, and invest for the African century.

Nzagha is a Long Africa permanent capital vehicle for investing and re‑investing on the continent. We are neither a classic VC nor a venture studio; we are a **flexible operating conglomerate organization** that invests, builds, co-creates, acquires, and scales new ventures.
